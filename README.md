settled — the one so foundational nobody reopens it — and refuse it. Then
build what follows.

1. **What I inverted** — the assumption, then the flip
2. **What it shows** — numbers, with method beside them
3. **What it doesn't show** — never softened, never moved

Five hundred words each. Anyone wanting more clicks the repo.

## Evidence

- **Latent Ocean** — 554 tests on the relational successor; 50 on the
  ternary successor, energy factor 6.99 pinned by a zero-dependency golden
  test. 1,404,816 measured triads across 10 corpora. Five deployed estates,
  including an industrial group with an export-controlled programme provably
  sealed. Five absence classes; three shrink with effort, two never do.
- **BTUT** — constant 12-iteration convergence from 500 to 10,000 agents.
  20–105× existing mean-field-game solver frameworks. β ≈ 0.5, γ_c ≈ 1.33.
  Six live domains: SUMO traffic (800 vehicles, zero gridlock), ROS, drone
  swarms, [franklinstreetdata.com](https://www.franklinstreetdata.com),
  [bigdunc.com](https://www.bigdunc.com), four cloud surfaces.
- **Crystara** — +36.6 AUC over baseline JEPA on CSET semiconductor; beats
  supervised GAT, GCN, GraphSAGE there. Loses to GAT on GDELT. 16 modules
  discovered 1-to-1 with real industry clusters, no labels.
- **NIV** — ROC-AUC 0.8538 at 18 months across 504 months (1970–2024)
  against ~0.72 for the 10Y/3M spread. 41.71% orthogonal variance.
  [SSRN 6664138](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6664138).
- **Participatory Data Estate** — SGUNCCH live: 40 policies, 8 departments,
  time-constant auth, RLS on every table, public approval ledger.

## Editorial policy

Every number resolves to a repository, a PDF, or a live system. A skeptic
should settle any doubt by clicking, never by asking.

Section 3 of every chapter states what is not established. It is never
softened and never moved below the fold. It is the reason the rest is
credible.
